    Connection settings

    The signaling server only introduces players to each other (table codes). Leave empty to use the default; without one, connect with invite codes.

    Players connect directly using WebRTC. STUN finds a direct route; if a firewall blocks it, a TURN relay is needed. Add your own TURN server here (stored on this device only).

    How to play

    110 cards: two decks plus 6 jokers. Everyone gets 13 cards. Get rid of all your cards first.

    Your turn

    1. Draw – tap the stock. Once you are out, you may take the top card of the discard pile instead.
    2. Meld (optional) – select cards and tap Meld.
    3. Discard – select one card and tap the discard pile.

    Melds

    Set: 3–4 cards of the same rank, all different suits (7♥ 7♠ 7♦).
    Run: 3+ cards in a row, same suit (4♣ 5♣ 6♣). Ace is low (A‑2‑3) or high (Q‑K‑A).
    Jokers replace any card.

    Coming out

    Your first meld must be worth at least 40 points (2–9 face value, 10/J/Q/K = 10, ace = 11 or 1 in A‑2‑3). Stage several groups and tap Lay down. Once you are out — and only on your turn — you may take from the discard pile, add cards to any meld on the table (select cards, tap the meld) and swap a joker for the card it stands for if you hold that card.

    Scoring

    When someone goes out, the others score their remaining cards as penalty: joker 20, ace 11, pictures 10, others face value. Going out in a single turn (Hand‑Rommé) doubles the penalty. Lowest total wins.

    Touch tips

    Tap to select. Drag a card to rearrange your hand, or drag it onto a meld or the discard pile.
